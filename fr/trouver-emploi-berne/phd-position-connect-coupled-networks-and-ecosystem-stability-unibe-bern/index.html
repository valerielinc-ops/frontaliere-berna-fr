Job Description

Towards a General Theory of Stability and Persistence in Spatially Coupled Food Webs A fully funded PhD position, supervised by Prof. Remo Ryser, is available in the newly established Network Ecology Division at the Institute of Ecology and Evolution, University of Bern, Switzerland, in collaboration with Dr. Isabelle Gounand (Sorbonne University, Paris). The position is part of the meta-network project of the group, in which we develop a general theory of how spatial networks interact with networks of species interactions to shape ecological patterns and functioning. See our homepage for examples. CONNECT builds on an established, empirically grounded bioenergetic meta-food-web model based on the Allometric Trophic Network (ATN) framework and uses systematic simulation experiments to identify general relationships between spatial network structure, dispersal, and ecological stability. The project is theoretical and simulation-based in nature, addressing following topics:

  • Spatial network theory: How does the structure of spatial networks, such as patch connectivity, degree distribution, and spatial embedding, interact with dispersal to shape the persistence and stability of complex food webs? Moving beyond single-aspect manipulations of landscape structure, this part of the project develops a systematic, network-theoretic account of space in meta-food-webs, incorporating biologically realistic, trait-based dispersal.
  • Beyond emergent patterns - fine-scale and transient dynamics: What mechanisms and short-term dynamics underlie emergent patterns such as diversity and persistence?
  • Towards a theory of spatial restoration: Can the resulting framework identify keystone patches and dispersal links, and guide the conservation and restoration of spatially structured ecosystems, for example by testing theoretical predictions against more realistic landscape structures?

Some of these questions should be addressed, but there is also room to develop own research questions in addition to these general directions. Your Profile
