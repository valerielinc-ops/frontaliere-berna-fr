Be a force for good and help nurture a healthier generation

As an Administrative Assistant at NPTC Nutrition & Health , your mission is to provide proactive, trusted administrative and organisational support to the Head of our Technical Competency Unit, while delivering outstanding events, visitor experiences and internal communications. At our Research and Development Center in Konolfingen, Switzerland, we develop science-proven nutrition products, processes and packaging, while providing technical support to Nestlé factories worldwide. You will make a difference by collaborating closely with senior leaders, colleagues across our teams, other local assistants, and external partners and visitors. This is your opportunity to grow your expertise and deliver meaningful results that help improve lives through nutrition innovation worldwide.

How you’ll make an impact as an Administrative Assistant

  • Empowering our leadership team by managing calendars, meetings and priorities, so they can focus on what matters most.
  • Creating memorable visits, workshops and events that showcase our science-based approach to nutrition for guests and partners.
  • Bringing our stories to life through engaging internal communications that keep around 100 colleagues informed and connected.
  • Welcoming newcomers and supporting colleagues with smooth onboarding and accurate, up-to-date information.
  • Leading small team initiatives and celebrations that strengthen community and company culture.
  • Improving administrative processes and service quality, so the whole team works more efficiently.

What will make you successful

  • Bringing strong planning and prioritisation skills, with an anticipatory approach and reliable delivery under changing deadlines.
  • Applying sound judgement and proactive problem-solving to work independently in a complex environment.
  • Building trust and collaborating across teams and organisational levels, while handling sensitive information with discretion.
  • Using clear business writing and digital tools to coordinate events, manage content and keep accurate records.
  • Communicating fluently in English
  • German is a big advantage.
  • Demonstrating courage, collaboration, speed and continuous learning, while translating ideas into measurable impact and creating shared value.

At Nestlé Nutrition, we place people at the heart of everything we do

What to expect from our hiring process

We are committed to an inclusive and respectful hiring experience and are happy to provide reasonable accommodations if needed. Our workplaces are fully accessible and supported by a strong safety culture, with modern installations and procedures to ensure a safe environment for everyone.

The hiring process typically includes an interview with Talent Acquisition, an interview with the hiring team, and a final discussion and offer. We review applications as they are received and close the role once a strong list of relevant candidates is identified, so we encourage you to apply early. All information is treated confidentially.

Stay vigilant: Nestlé will only contact candidates via official company email addresses and will never ask for payments or sensitive personal information at any stage of the hiring process.
