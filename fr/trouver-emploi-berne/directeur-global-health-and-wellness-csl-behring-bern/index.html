Vue d’ensemble du poste

The Opportunity

The Global Occupational Health & Wellness Director is responsible for designing, governing, and continuously improving a global strategy and operating model for Occupational Health (OH) including Injury and Workers’ Compensation case management, Employee wellness, and Occupational Health Protection (IH).

This is a high visibility and impact role that ensures compliance, protects and promotes worker health, reduces injury-related risk, and supports business performance across all global operations.

  • The Opportunity:
  • The Global Occupational Health & Wellness Director is responsible for designing, governing, and continuously improving a global strategy and operating model for Occupational Health (OH) including Injury and Workers’ Compensation case management, Employee wellness, and Occupational Health Protection (IH).

Entreprise et contexte

  • Serve as a global liaison across EHS, HR, Legal/Privacy, Risk/Insurance, Business Continuity, Facilities, and site leadership to develop and implement fit-for-purpose plans that advance organizational objectives.
  • Conduct strategic assessments of operating models, including resourcing and Health & Wellness functions, to identify and implement solutions that optimize efficiency through the effective use of internal and external resources. The Role:
  • Develop and implement a global Occ Health & Wellness strategy aligned with EHS vision.
  • Lead Injury Management and Workers’ Compensation programs across the US, Europe and Australia, ensuring compliant handling, medical quality, and efficient claim resolution.
  • Establish global standards, clinical protocols, and IH governance systems.
  • Lead global case management including early communication, functional capacity evaluations, and accommodation assessments.
  • Govern Australian self-insurance injury management systems and compliance audits.
  • Oversee international Early Intervention programs focusing on rapid triage and injury minimization, capacity and “workability” function retention.
  • Lead OH/IH global teams and manage external vendor performance.
  • Own global Occupational Health data systems, KPIs, and reporting. Skills & Experience (preferred):
